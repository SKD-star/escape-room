# 🗝 WALKTHROUGH — How to Clear All 10 Levels

> ⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING** — this guide explains how to beat every room and
> unlock the best ending. Play blind first if you want the real experience!

---

## The Basics (read this first)

Every room works the same way — **follow the diamonds**:

1. **Explore** — walk around, read every note (press `E` on papers/books).
   Your flashlight starts raised in room 1; press `F` to toggle it any time.
2. **Find the gold diamond 🔶** — a glowing gold marker floats and spins above
   each room's **puzzle anchor** (a lectern, altar, keypad, terminal…). Walk
   up to it; when the crosshair turns **gold** and shows `E Interact`, press
   `E` to open the puzzle. Aiming is forgiving — near the object is enough,
   you don't need pixel-perfect precision.
3. **Solve the puzzle** — it is one of 3 types (AI picks randomly each run):

   | Type | How to solve |
   |---|---|
   | **Keypad** | Enter the digit code. The code is in the puzzle's own **clue text** — read the narrative carefully, or use hints. |
   | **Riddle** | Type the answer word (one word, e.g. `echo`, `fire`, `shadow`, `map`). Close spellings count. |
   | **Sequence** | Click the symbols in the correct order. The clue always tells you which symbol is FIRST. |

4. **Follow the green diamond 🟢** — when the puzzle is solved you hear the
   door unseal, the gold marker vanishes, and a **green marker** appears over
   the exit door. Walk to it and press `E` to open the door and move on.

**Collecting things:** key items shimmer with a soft gold glow, notes are
paper sheets on surfaces, and each room hides a battery in a corner. Aim
roughly at any of them, wait for the gold crosshair + label, press `E`.
`Tab` opens your inventory to check what you're carrying.

**Stuck? Press "Request Hint" in the puzzle window.** Hints come in 3 levels:
- Hint 1 = cryptic nudge
- Hint 2 = clear direction
- Hint 3 = almost gives you the answer (for riddles it tells you the first
  letter and length!)

> ⚠️ But don't overuse hints — more than **12 hints total** across the run
> locks you into the **Dark Ending** (see the endings section).

### Difficulty modes

| Mode | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Story** | The presence never manifests, sanity is gentle, batteries last twice as long. **No room clock.** Score ×0.75. |
| **Normal** | The intended experience. A **generous room countdown** (soft — overtime just drains sanity, never fails). Score ×1. |
| **Nightmare** | The presence manifests ~2× as often, batteries drain fast, sanity crumbles. The **room countdown is a hard deadline — run it out and the room restarts** (fresh puzzle, full timer; cleared rooms are kept). Score ×1.5. |

> ⏳ The countdown sits at the top of the screen and keeps ticking while you work
> the lock (it pauses in menus, notes and dialogue). Per-room Nightmare limits
> and full details are in the in-game **Manual → Chapters**. Turn it off in
> **Settings → Controls → Room Countdown**.

### Survival layer

- **🔦 Flashlight (`F`)** — auto-raised when you start; drains battery while
  on (~1 minute of continuous light). You always find a partial battery at
  each room's entrance, and **every room hides one full battery cell in a
  corner** (+45% charge — look for the faint gold glow). The beam flickers
  when the battery is low… or when *something* is close.
- **🧠 Sanity (eye icon, bottom-left)** — standing in darkness drains it; the
  presence drains it faster. Stay near candles/torches or keep the flashlight
  on to recover. Solving puzzles (+18), escaping rooms (+25) and finding
  secrets (+10) also restore it. Low sanity distorts your vision, quickens
  your heartbeat and invites whispers — and if it ever hits rock-bottom (≤5),
  the run is marked for the **Dark Ending**.
- **👁 The Presence** — a dark figure that manifests every few minutes
  (more often in later rooms). It drifts toward you. **Hold it in the
  flashlight beam for ~2 seconds to banish it** (unlocks the secret
  *Light Bearer* achievement). If it touches you, you lose 22 sanity.
  It never kills you — it just makes everything worse.
  **Stealth matters:** sprinting makes noise and draws it out sooner;
  crouching (and standing still) keeps you quiet and slows its hunt.
  (Both systems can be turned off in Settings → Controls if you want a
  pure puzzle experience.)

## Room-by-Room Guide

> In every room below, the **puzzle anchor** is the object under the floating
> **gold diamond** — you can't miss it. After solving, follow the **green
> diamond** to the exit.

### Chapter I — The Haunted Library
- **Story note:** on the reading table in the center ("The Librarian's Last Entry").
- **KEY ITEM 🗝:** the **Brass Key** lies on the floor on the right side of the
  room (near the far right wall, close to the alcove area). *You need this for the True Ending!*
- **SECRET 🏆:** on the LEFT wall between the bookshelves is a **hidden lever**.
  Pull it — a bookshelf on the right grinds aside (unlocks the *Behind the
  Walls* achievement).
- **Ghost:** the floating Librarian drifts through the room — press `E` to talk
  to her. Ask her about the room; she drops puzzle guidance.
- **Puzzle anchor:** the tall **lectern** with a candle at the far end (north side).
- **Exit:** the big door on the north wall.

### Chapter II — The Ancient Temple
- **Story note:** "Carved Warning" on the central altar.
- **KEY ITEM 🐍:** the **Serpent Idol** on the floor in the far-left corner.
- **Ghost:** The Keeper floats on the right side.
- **Puzzle anchor:** the **stone altar** with the fire brazier in the center.
- Sequence puzzles are common here — remember: *"the offering remembers"* —
  the clue names the first symbol.

### Chapter III — The Forgotten Prison
- **Story note:** "Incident Report — Cell 3" on the interrogation table.
- **KEY ITEM 🗝:** the **Warden's Key** is INSIDE cell 3 (the cell with the
  dark open gap in the bars, front-center). Walk in and grab it.
- **Ghost:** Prisoner 47 haunts the left cells.
- **Puzzle anchor:** the **keypad** on the warden's desk (back-left corner,
  tilted on the table).
- The swinging lamp is the only light — follow it if you're lost.

### Chapter IV — The Abandoned Laboratory
- **Story note:** "Research Log — Day 214" on the rear lab bench.
- **KEY ITEM 🧪:** the **Serum Vial** sits on a tank pedestal on the left wall
  (between the glowing green specimen tanks).
- **Ghost:** Dr. Halvorsen paces the right side.
- **Puzzle anchor:** the **terminal with the green screen** in the right corner.
- The fluorescent light flickers and dies at random — that's normal. Keep going.

### Chapter V — The Abandoned Hospital
- **Story note:** "Patient Chart — East Ward" on a gurney in the ward rows.
- **KEY ITEM 🏷:** the **Morgue Tag** lies on the overturned gurney on the
  right side of the room.
- **Ghost:** The Night Nurse patrols the left side.
- **Puzzle anchor:** the **morgue drawer wall** (3×3 metal drawers) on the
  back wall.

### Chapter VI — The Haunted Mansion
- **Story note:** "The Heir's Confession" on the dining table.
- **KEY ITEM 📿:** the **Silver Locket** is on the grand staircase (left side
  of the room, on one of the steps). *You need this for the True Ending!*
- **SECRET:** press `E` on the **mirror** on the right wall… if you dare.
- **Ghost:** The Lady drifts near the dining area.
- **Puzzle anchor:** the **grandfather clock** in the far-left corner
  (stopped at 3:47 — this number matters across the whole story).
- **➤ TRUE ENDING STEP:** open `Tab` inventory, click **Brass Key**, then click
  **Silver Locket** → they combine into the **Locketed Key (memento)**. Done —
  the True Ending is now secured!

### Chapter VII — The Medieval Castle
- **Story note:** "The Last Verdict" on the round table.
- **KEY ITEM 👑:** the **Royal Seal** on the floor on the right side.
- **Ghost:** The Undying King floats above the throne (he's bigger than the
  others — talk to him, he's chatty for a 600-year-old corpse).
- **Puzzle anchor:** the **golden crest** lying on the round table in the center.
- The banner colors from the note ("crimson kneels before azure, azure before
  gold") hint at sequence order.

### Chapter VIII — The Secret Bunker
- **Story note:** "Final Transmission — 0347 hours" near the bunk beds (left wall).
- **KEY ITEM 📕:** the **Cipher Codebook** is hidden UNDER the bunk beds —
  crouch (`C`) and look at the floor beneath them. The note tells you:
  *"I hid it instead. Under where we sleep."*
- **Ghost:** The Operator stands near the crates.
- **Puzzle anchor:** the big **radio console wall** with blinking lights (back wall).
- Don't bother with the hatch wheel on the right… or do. (It's not the way out.)

### Chapter IX — The Cyber AI Facility
- **Story note:** read the left terminal ("SYSLOG — FRAGMENT 03:47:12" —
  yes, 3:47 again).
- **KEY ITEM 💾:** the **Neural Access Chip** on the floor at the far right, past
  the server racks.
- **Ghost:** PROCESS_0 glitches around the left side — it *teleports*, so chase it.
- **Puzzle anchor:** the huge **pulsing blue AI core** on the central pillar.
- **➤ ALTERNATE TRUE ENDING STEP:** if you missed the locket combo, combine
  **Cipher Codebook + Neural Access Chip** in your inventory → **Decoded Chip**.
  This ALSO unlocks the True Ending.

### Finale — The Final Convergence
- Ten glowing doors circle the room — one for every room you escaped.
- **Story note:** "The First Page (you wrote this)" near the pedestal. Read it.
- **The Convergence** (the giant eye) watches you. Talk to it (`E`) — it asks
  for "the answer you've carried since the library."
- **Puzzle anchor:** the **stone pedestal** with the glowing gold ring, in front
  of the eye. Solve the final puzzle.
- Walk through the exit door one last time → **your ending plays.**

## The Three Endings

| Ending | How to get it |
|---|---|
| 🌅 **Standard — "The Way Out"** | Just finish all 10 rooms. |
| 👑 **True — "The Whole Truth"** (150 pts) | Finish holding the **Locketed Key** (Brass Key + Silver Locket) **or** the **Decoded Chip** (Codebook + Access Chip). Combine in `Tab` inventory before finishing the boss room! |
| 💀 **Dark — "The Eleventh Room"** | Finish having used **more than 12 hints**, or let your **sanity hit ≤5** at any point during the run. (Avoid this by hint-rationing and staying in the light — or seek it deliberately, it's an achievement of sorts.) |

## Speedrun / Score Tips

Leaderboard score = `(rooms×1000 + puzzles×250 − seconds/6 − hints×100) × difficulty`
(difficulty multiplier: Story ×0.75 · Normal ×1 · Nightmare ×1.5)

- Sprint everywhere (watch the stamina bar), skip optional notes on repeat runs —
  but remember sprinting draws the presence out sooner on Normal/Nightmare.
- Zero hints. Riddle answers repeat between runs — learn the pool
  (`keyboard, hole, echo, fire, shadow, map, stamp, future`…).
- Keypad codes are IN the clue text — read before guessing.
- Save with `F5` before attempting a puzzle if you're worried about time.
- Turn on **Settings → Controls → Speedrun Timer** for a live clock with
  per-room personal-best splits.
- Nightmare pays ×1.5 score — worth it once you know the rooms.

### 0. Authored answers — one fixed puzzle per level

admin room uses its theme's answer (e.g. an added "cyber" room → `machine`).

| The Haunted Library *(library)* | The Overdue Ledger **or** The Librarian's Whisper | keypad / riddle | **`1847`** or **`book`** |
| The Ancient Temple *(temple)* | The Rite of Serpents | sequence | **serpent → moon → eye → flame → skull** |
| The Forgotten Prison *(prison)* | The Warden's Tally | keypad | **`0713`** |

### 2. Keypads — where the code is

The digit code is **random every run**, so there is no fixed number — but it
is never hidden from you:

1. **Read the puzzle's own clue text** — the narrative on the keypad screen
   contains or points to the code.
2. Wait ~45 seconds in the room and a **whisper toast** repeats the clue.
3. Still stuck? Press **Request Hint** twice — **Hint 3 (tier 2) walks you to
   the answer**. Codes are 3–6 digits (longer on Nightmare).
4. There is no attempt limit — failed entries just shake the display. You
   can also brute-force short codes if you enjoy pain.

### 3. Sequences — how to read the order

- The room shows 4–8 glowing symbols from this set:
  ☾ moon · ◉ eye · § serpent · ⚿ key · ☠ skull · ♦ flame · ⧗ hourglass · ♠ raven
- **The clue always names the FIRST symbol** ("The moon leads…").
- The buttons are shuffled on screen — the display order is NOT the answer.
- **Hint tier 2 essentially spells out the order.** Wrong picks reset the
  attempt with no penalty besides time, so you can also solve by elimination:
  lock the known first symbol, then test one position at a time.

### 4. The two item combinations (True Ending)

Open `Tab`, click the first item, then the second:

| Combine | + | = | Found in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brass Key** | **Silver Locket** | 🔐 **Locketed Key** | Library (Ch. I) + Mansion (Ch. VI) |
| **Cipher Codebook** | **Neural Access Chip** | 🧩 **Decoded Chip** | Bunker (Ch. VIII) + Cyber Facility (Ch. IX) |

Carrying **either** result into the finale locks in the **True Ending**.
All other pairs answer *"These refuse each other."* — only these two work.

### 5. Quick answers to common questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is there one "master key" that opens everything? | No single item — the **gold diamond puzzle** is the only lock per room. The Locketed Key / Decoded Chip are ending items, not door openers. |
| Do riddle answers change between runs? | The pool is fixed (table above); which riddle you get is random. |
| Does 3:47 unlock anything? | It's the story's recurring motif (clock, lab, syslog). If a keypad clue references "the time it stopped," try **347** / **0347**. |
| Max hints before Dark Ending? | 12 total across the run. 13+ = Dark Ending. |
| Can the presence kill me? | Never — it only drains sanity (22 per touch). Only sanity ≤5 has ending consequences. |
| Fastest way to True Ending? | Grab Brass Key (Ch. I) + Silver Locket (Ch. VI), combine immediately, finish the game. |

|---|---|
| **Can't interact / `E` does nothing** | Walk closer (range ≈ 3.4 m) and center the crosshair on the object until it turns **gold** and shows `E Interact`. Aim assist helps, but you must be near enough. |

| **Game won't start** | From the project folder run `npx kill-port 3000 3001 5000` then `npm start`, and open **localhost:3000**. |
